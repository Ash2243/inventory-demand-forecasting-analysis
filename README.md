# inventory-demand-forecasting-analysis
End-to-end inventory optimization and demand forecasting analysis using real-world retail wine &amp; spirits sales, procurement, and inventory datasets.


## Project Overview

This project analyzes large-scale retail wine & spirits inventory, procurement, and sales datasets to identify opportunities for inventory optimization, demand forecasting, and supply chain improvement.

The analysis focuses on improving stock management efficiency, reducing inventory-related financial risks, and extracting business insights from transactional retail data.

## Business Objectives

- Optimize inventory allocation and replenishment
- Identify high-performing and slow-moving products
- Perform ABC inventory classification
- Analyze supplier and procurement efficiency
- Forecast sales demand using time-series analysis
- Reduce stockout and overstock risks
- Improve operational decision-making using data-driven insights

## Datasets Used

1. Product & Pricing Data
2. Beginning Inventory Snapshot
3. Ending Inventory Snapshot
4. Vendor Invoice & Freight Data
5. Purchase Transactions
6. Sales Transactions

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Scikit-learn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Analyses Performed

- Data Cleaning & Validation
- Inventory Valuation Analysis
- Store-Level Inventory Analysis
- ABC Inventory Classification
- Demand Forecasting using Moving Average
- Inventory Turnover Analysis
- Procurement Lead-Time Analysis
- Freight Cost Analysis
- Reorder Point Calculation
- Vendor Performance Analysis

## Major Business Insights

- A-category products contributed the majority of sales revenue despite representing a smaller subset of SKUs.
- Several products exhibited extremely high inventory turnover, indicating strong demand but potential stockout risk.
- Certain slow-moving products showed significant overstock exposure and capital lock-in.
- Procurement lead times remained relatively stable, enabling predictable replenishment cycles.
- Demand variability was high, reinforcing the importance of dynamic forecasting and adaptive inventory policies.
